**Unofficial DMZ Addon** expands **DragonMineZ** with new race progression, transformations, classes, minigames, custom forms, visuals, combat mechanics, and Dragon Ball-style space travel. Every feature is designed around DragonMineZ's native skill trees, menus, controls, progression, and configuration systems.

### Current features

* **Alien race** with native character customization, the exclusive **Adaptive Physiology** passive, and the **Full Power** transformation. Repeated exposure builds up to 20% resistance to one damage type. Aliens are a biological race and cannot become Androids.
* **Four new classes for every race**: Ki Adept, Duelist, Vanguard, and Tactician. Their stats and defense growth follow DragonMineZ's class balance and remain configurable per race.
* **Custom Forms** created from a dedicated DragonMineZ character-menu tab. The paged creator includes a live 3D preview; individual eye, hair, body, aura, and Saiyan tail colors; selectable hair; configurable Ki drain and power; and server-enforced TP pricing. Players begin with one slot and earn configurable additional slots by completing DragonMineZ sagas.
* **Snake minigame** in DragonMineZ's minigames menu. It unlocks after the player earns a non-innate race transformation, awards TP for collected apples, and becomes faster as the snake grows.

### Transformations and progression

* **Universal Ultra Instinct** for every race: Sign, Mastered, and True Ultra Instinct. Successful dodges cancel incoming attacks and use synchronized effects with DragonMineZ's native randomized evasion animations. Dodge reliability improves with each tier, while exhaustion is balanced separately per form.
* **Universal Ultra Ego** for every race as one independent form. Taking damage progressively raises its stats for the current transformation, resetting on death or detransformation, while its aggressive purple aura bursts outward when the user is hit.
* **Saiyan forms**: Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue, Super Saiyan Rosé, Super Saiyan Blue Evolved, Super Saiyan Rosé Evolved, Super Saiyan Rage, and Beast. Blue and Blue Evolved belong to the non-Evil path; Rosé and Rosé Evolved are their Evil-path counterparts. Blue can combine its native blue visuals with a separate Kaioken overlay, while Blue Evolved and Rosé Evolved do not stack with Kaioken.
* **Race forms**: Namekian Orange, Frost Demon Golden and Black, and Alien Full Power.
* **Ascending endgame balance**: Blue Evolved and Rosé Evolved lead into Ultra Instinct Sign, Mastered Ultra Instinct, True Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego, Orange, Black, and finally Beast. Their shared mastery scale preserves that order at every mastery level.
* Addon transformations unlock through the appropriate native race skill trees with TP costs, prerequisites, and mastery requirements.
* Every addon transformation is selectable through DragonMineZ's native **X-key transformation wheel**, including dedicated God Forms, Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego, and Custom Forms sections.
* Independent forms cannot activate over conflicting transformations. Hairless races receive equivalent body-color changes while preserving ears, antennae, and other native 3D head parts.
* Exact transformation benefits are shown in their skill descriptions without transformation chat spam.

### Combat, auras, and settings

* UI and MUI use a subtle heat-haze effect, TUI uses a persistent flame aura when enabled, and Ultra Ego produces a hit-reactive aura burst.
* God-form auras remain stable while using Ki techniques. **Constant Addon Auras** controls passive addon auras without suppressing effects while charging Ki or using DragonMineZ's Aura Status.
* **Halal Mode**, subtitled "For my muslim fellows", swaps the whole words God/god with Frog/frog in localized text.
* Form multipliers, UI dodging and exhaustion, special buffs, Alien mechanics, aura behavior, custom-form limits and pricing, and Space systems are configurable for players, servers, and modpacks.

### Dragon Ball-style Space

* Enter Space through DragonMineZ's native space-pod menu after unlocking **Fly**. Piloted pods travel at triple normal flight speed, remain stable in zero gravity, and carry their rider between destinations without duplication.
* The animated Solar System contains a bright Sun, rotating cubic planets, orbital movement, parallax stars, motion feedback, and an anime-style sky.
* Earth, Namek, and Sacred Kai's Planet connect to their DragonMineZ dimensions. Approaching an unlocked planet transfers the player safely to solid ground, while returning uses the player's last departure position.
* Destination access is enforced per player. Locked worlds appear as pitch-black cubes with a floating camera-facing question mark and cannot be entered or destroyed until that player meets the native unlock condition.
* Ki attacks keep their native DragonMineZ trajectories in Space and can destroy unlocked planets. Planets fade out with a centered explosion and reform after five minutes.
* North Galaxy and Universe 7 use separate Space dimensions connected through visible two-way gateways. Configurable boundaries keep players and pods within navigable areas without closing the Solar System horizontally.
* A compact crosshair HUD provides wrapped information about planets, galaxies, universes, access state, distance, environment, and travel guidance.

DragonMineZ is required. Configuration files are generated in the normal DragonMineZ and Unofficial DMZ Addon configuration directories for pack authors and server owners.
