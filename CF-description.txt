**Unofficial DMZ Addon 10.3.0** expands **DragonMine Z 2.1.3** with transformations, race progression, visuals, and mechanics built around DragonMineZ's native systems.

### Current features

* **Alien race**, with native character customization, the exclusive **Adaptive Physiology** passive, and the **Full Power** transformation. Repeated exposure builds up to 20% resistance to one damage type; Aliens are a distinct biological path and cannot become Androids.
* **Dragon Ball-style Space**, unlocked through DragonMineZ's **Fly** skill and accessible from the native space-pod menu. Space provides visible weightless flight, no Earth weather or clouds, a seamless anime sky, shared rotating solar systems, independently spinning cubic worlds with GPT Image 2 surface art, a bright textured sun, reliable high-speed planet travel, native DMZ ki trajectories with accurate planet impacts, Evil-path consequences, animated explosions, and five-minute world reformation.
* **Persistent space-pod travel**. Piloted pods move at triple normal flight speed, close their doors during travel, and carry their rider into planets without duplication. The source pod is removed during transfer, the rider stays seated in the destination pod, and each planet returns the player to their last departure position. Players traveling without a pod arrive without one, while unoccupied pods left in Space remain saved and stationary without falling.
* **Per-player progression-aware planets**. Worlds locked by DragonMineZ progression, including the Sacred Kai Dimension, appear as pitch-black cubes with a floating camera-facing question mark and cannot be entered or destroyed until that specific player meets the native unlock condition. Multiplayer clients share planet positions without sharing progression permissions.
* **Planet analysis HUD**. Aim at a world to see its classification, access state, distance, orbital radius, atmosphere, environment, destination, and travel guidance. Locked planets keep their identity and telemetry hidden.
* **Optional renderer compatibility**. A depth-safe late celestial pass supports Oculus/Iris and OptiFine-style shader pipelines, while Distant Horizons is disabled through its API only inside the empty Space dimension and restored on exit. Voxy and other LOD stages are isolated from the custom celestial renderer without becoming required dependencies.
* **Safe planetary arrivals** that locate solid, dry ground with enough headroom instead of spawning players underground.
* **Four new native classes for every race**: Ki Adept, Duelist, Vanguard, and Tactician. Their base stats, scaling, and passive values are stored per race in DragonMineZ `stats.json` files for modpack tuning.
* **Universal Ultra Instinct progression** for every race: Sign, Mastered, and True Ultra Instinct. Successful dodges fully cancel incoming attacks and trigger synchronized particles, sound, displacement, and DragonMineZ's native randomized evasion animations. Dodge chance scales with form and mastery: **Sign 50-70%**, **Mastered 65-82%**, and **True 88-97%**. Higher tiers dodge farther and consume less energy.
* **Universal Ultra Ego** for every race as one lore-accurate form. UI has no persistent aura, while UE keeps its aggressive purple aura without changing Aura Status; persistent God Form auras survive Ki techniques without flashing.
* **Saiyan transformations**: Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue, Super Saiyan Rosé, Super Saiyan Blue Evolved, Super Saiyan Rage, and Beast. Blue preserves its blue hair and aura beneath a separate red **Kaioken** overlay; Blue Evolved matches Blue with Kaioken x20 power and cannot stack with Kaioken.
* **Race transformations** including Namekian Orange, Frost Demon Golden and Black, and Alien Full Power.
* Transformation unlocks integrated into the appropriate native race skill trees with TP costs, mastery requirements, and prerequisite forms.
* Every addon transformation is selectable through DragonMineZ's native **X-key transformation wheel**, including dedicated Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego sections.
* Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego, Beast, Orange, Golden, Black, and Alien Full Power are independent forms and cannot activate while a conflicting transformation is active.
* Hairless races display Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego colors through their bodies while preserving ears and other native 3D head components.
* Every active form receives combat benefits. Exact buffs are documented in the relevant skill descriptions without transformation chat spam.
* Existing DragonMineZ Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego forms and icons are reused rather than duplicated, and their values remain replaceable through DragonMineZ configuration files.

DragonMineZ is required. Configuration files are generated under the normal DragonMineZ and Unofficial DMZ Addon config directories. Pack authors and server owners can toggle individual addon forms, dodges, auras, buffs, Alien passive behavior, Space systems, Fly requirements, planet interaction, the planet HUD, shader/LOD compatibility, floor height, pod speed, collision margins, and arrival distance. UI and UE unlock from native race form skill trees rather than Goku or Vegeta.
