Unofficial DMZ Addon 10.2.1

Space
- Space is accessible from DragonMineZ's space-pod menu after learning the Fly skill
- Space uses weightless flight, a seamless Dragon Ball-inspired sky, and a safety floor that prevents players and pods from falling into the void
- Only actual planetary dimensions appear as nearby, separated worlds; approaching a world travels to its dimension
- Piloted space pods move at triple normal flight speed and can enter planets while keeping the rider mounted
- Traveling into a planet without a pod now leaves the player without a pod on arrival
- Unoccupied pods left in Space remain saved there, while a ridden pod transfers without leaving a duplicate behind
- Space pod doors close correctly when entering or traveling through Space
- Planet arrivals now search for solid, dry ground with enough headroom instead of placing players underground
- Planets can be destroyed with the owner's ki attacks, fade out with an explosion, and reform after five minutes
- Fixed the world-travel crash caused by modifying Space's active player list during iteration

Ultra Instinct
- Successful Ultra Instinct dodges now cancel the attack before damage, hurt animation, damage numbers, or invulnerability frames are applied
- Increased dodge frequency based on the active form and its mastery: Sign 50-70%, Mastered 65-82%, Autonomous 78-91%, and True 88-97%
- Higher Ultra Instinct forms dodge farther, gain more vertical lift, and consume less energy per dodge
- Dodge movement now uses authoritative server velocity so normal movement input cannot suppress it
- Added synchronized particles, sound, and a brief alternating 45-degree body lean when a dodge succeeds
