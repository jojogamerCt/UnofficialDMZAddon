Unofficial DMZ Addon 10.2.4

Space and planet travel
- Moved space arrivals close to the source planet while retaining a short arrival grace period.
- Matched travel and ki-impact collision to the planets' cubic geometry, preventing players and fast pods from phasing through corners.
- Made DragonMineZ space-pod transfers atomic: departure state is captured before the source pod is removed, the rider spawns beside the correct source planet inside one closed destination pod, and no pod is lost or duplicated.
- Added persistent per-planet return points so players arrive at the exact position and facing direction where they last departed that dimension for Space.
- Restored player visibility during space flight and retained DragonMineZ ki projectile behavior in the active Space dimension.
- Kept the invisible space floor and pod speed configurable, alongside planet travel, destruction, entry margins, and arrival clearance.
- Removed gravity and automatic orbit motion from space pods. Unoccupied pods now remain completely stationary in Space.
- Prevented muscular or scaled race bodies from blocking the first-person camera while seated inside a space pod.
- Fixed repeat pod journeys from a planet to Space after returning from a previous trip by validating the Fly skill directly instead of relying on stale cross-dimension tag state.
- Fixed the production-client startup crash caused by the stationary space-pod mixin targeting a development-only method name.

Alien race
- Replaced Blood-Fueled Ki with Adaptive Physiology, an Alien-exclusive passive that builds 4% resistance per repeated damage type up to 20% and resets when adapting to a different threat.
- Assigned Alien its own racial identity instead of inheriting Human mechanics.
- Removed Android transformation eligibility from Alien configs and clears stale Android upgrades from existing Alien characters.

New classes
- Added Ki Adept, Duelist, Vanguard, and Tactician to DragonMineZ's native class selector for every race.
- Gave every addon class a distinct name color in class-selection and character-information screens.
- Added distinct configurable stat growth and passives for sustained Ki, precision offense, defensive recovery, and alternating melee/Ki combat.
- Stores the new class definitions in each race's DragonMineZ stats.json so servers and modpacks can tune them independently.
Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego
- Removed Autonomous Ultra Instinct; True Ultra Instinct now progresses directly from Mastered Ultra Instinct.
- Consolidated Ultra Ego Sign and Mastered Ultra Ego into one lore-accurate Ultra Ego form.
- Renamed the remaining Mastered Ultra Ego entry to Ultra Ego and corrected UI/UE skill descriptions.
- Migrated previous four-tier UI and two-tier UE costs into the new three-tier/one-tier trees, preventing priceless forms.
- Preserved previous AUI/TUI and UE Sign/Mastered purchases by mapping their skill levels and mastery to TUI and Ultra Ego.
- Removed persistent aura rendering from every Ultra Instinct tier.
- Kept Ultra Ego's aggressive purple aura persistent without changing DragonMineZ Aura Status.
- Removed Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego from Goku and Vegeta's offerings; both now unlock only through native race form skill trees or administrator commands.

God Forms and progression
- Stopped persistent God aura rendering from repeatedly rewriting Aura Status, eliminating Ki-move aura flashing.
- Fixed alignment-aware transformation cycling: Good characters progress through Blue and Blue Evolved, while Evil characters progress through Rose.
- Enforced Super Saiyan Rose's Evil-path restriction in wheel selection and normal transformation cycling.
- Added Blue Evolved to migrated wheel layouts so buying or granting it becomes visible immediately.
- Preserved normal transformation-key progression beyond Super Saiyan Blue.

Configuration
- Expanded unofficialdmzaddon-common.toml with toggles for individual addon transformations, UI dodges, persistent divine auras, special form buffs, Alien's racial passive, Space, Fly requirements, planet travel/destruction, visibility recovery, floor height, pod speed, collision margins, and arrival clearance.
